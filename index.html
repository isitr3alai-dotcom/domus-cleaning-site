03 / Specialist service

Sunbed maintenance
& internal clean.

Salon and home sunbeds serviced properly: megaSun and every other make. We open it up, clean it inside as well as out, do all the filters, and get underneath the base where the dust, hair and lotion residue collect.

  • ✓Internal deep cleanCanopy and bench opened up so we clean the inside, not just the surfaces you can see. Inner shell, reflectors and lamp bays degreased and de-dusted.
  • ✓All filters, no exceptionsIntake, fan and canopy filters removed, washed or replaced where they're past it, then refitted properly so airflow and cooling are back to where they should be.
  • ✓Underneath the baseWe get under the bed and clean the base, the vents beneath it and the floor it stands on: the spot that gets skipped and where dust, hair and lotion build up.
  • ✓Acrylics & lampsAcrylic sheets cleaned both sides with sunbed-safe products, never solvents that craze acrylic, and tubes wiped down and checked as we go.
  • ✓Sanitised for the next clientBody-contact surfaces, headrest, handles and control panel sanitised and left ready to use.
  • ✓Salon or home, one bed or a shop floorScheduled around your opening hours, fully insured, quoted per job, with a regular service schedule if you want one.
